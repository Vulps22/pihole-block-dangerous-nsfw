# Code of Conduct

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Keep discussion on topic: whether a domain belongs on the list.
- **Do not post explicit content**, including screenshots of explicit chats, in issues or PRs. Describe it in words instead.
- Don't post personal information about anyone.

Maintainers may edit, hide or lock anything that breaks these rules, and may block repeat offenders.
